Price format and initial rules

CSV: time,open,high,low,close,volume. time is the candle opening time (ISO with timezone or Unix). Use at least 30 closed candles in chronological order for the selected asset and timeframe. volume is optional.

Experimental rule: close outside the previous 20-candle high/low, aligned with the 20-close moving average. A 14-candle ATR measures volatility. A breakout does not guarantee a profitable trade.

How this scanner works

A swing needs two closed candles on each side. Rising swing highs and lows indicate bullish structure; falling highs and lows indicate bearish structure. A BOS is the first close crossing a confirmed swing level. A retest is a subsequent candle touching that level within 30 minutes.

Directional frequencies use past cases with the same confirmed trend. Retest frequencies use past BOS events in each direction. Windows do not overlap within each statistic. Each percentage needs at least 30 completed outcomes; n is the sample size and CI is a Wilson confidence interval. Descriptive historical statistics, not a validated trading success rate. Order blocks are not identified by this scanner.
